## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-21

The first release.

- A page for every café: the front sketched from a photo (or drawn from memory), the usual, the spot, the barista, stamps and a story.
- A sugar packet for every café on a cork board under string lights: five prints, ten colourways, city stamps for cafés away from home.
- A ring for every cup, in the drink's colour, piling up at "my spot"; lines in pencil beside them, and every cup on a receipt.
- "The usual?": written down or learned from repeated orders; from the tenth visit the café is yours.
- An hour by the window: time counted while the phone lies face down.
- Packets for friends as plain text; shared back in, they land on the board as "want to go".
- Closed cafés keep their page, with the window gone dark and a last line.
- The year in cups, with a personal price index.
- The map: cafés as sugar packets on a sepia OpenStreetMap plan, by city; "I'm here" or pick on the map; packets carry the place.
- English and Russian. Online only for map tiles.
